# World Physics & State Vector

## The World State Vector
The Universe is defined by a multi-dimensional state vector.

### Primary Dimensions

#### 1. Entropy (`entropy`)
- **Definition**: The degree of disorder and randomness.
- **High (>0.8)**: War, Revolution, Anarchy, Creative Destruction.
- **Low (<0.2)**: Stagnation, Crystalization, Absolute Control.
- **Ideal**: ~0.4-0.6 (Dynamic Equilibrium).

#### 2. Order (`order`) / Structurality
- **Definition**: The rigidity of social and physical structures.
- **High**: Empire, Totalitarianism, Bureaucracy.
- **Low**: Tribalism, Nomadic life.

#### 3. Cohesion (`cohesion`)
- **Definition**: The strength of the "glue" holding the world together (Culture, Religion, Identity).
- **High**: Unity, Hivemind.
- **Low**: Civil War, Fragmentation.

#### 4. Stability Index (`stability_index`)
- **Derived Metric**: Calculated from the balance of Entropy, Order, and Cohesion.
- **Formula**: `(Order * Cohesion) / (Entropy + 0.1)` (Simplified).

## Physics Updates
The `BasePhysicsEngine` updates these values every tick based on:
1.  **Drift**: Natural tendency towards Entropy.
2.  **Interventions**: God Console actions (e.g., "Entropy Shock").
3.  **Material Effects**: Accumulated pressure from active materials.
